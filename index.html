      📑 Official Legal Process Guide

      FIR & Legal Recourse Assistant

      1. Call 1930 Helpline First 2-24 Hours

      Financial fraud hotline managed by Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting System (CFCFRMS). Directly alerts banks to freeze defrauded money in destination accounts.

      2. cybercrime.gov.in Portal Official Record

      National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. Generates an official Acknowledgement Number required by police and banks for formal tracking.

      3. Local Police Station FIR Formal Investigation

      Physical legal notice under IT Act / IPC (BNSS). Police issue formal notices under Sec 91/94 to banks & telecom operators to recover funds and trace culprits.

      📂 Required Documents for Filing FIR / Cyber Complaint:
      • Bank Statement (highlighted)
      • Government ID Proof (Aadhaar/PAN)
      • Fraudulent Transaction UTR Numbers
      • Mobile Call Logs & Chat Export
      • Cyber Crime Portal Ack Slip
      • Screenshots of Phishing Link/App
      • Suspect Phone / UPI VPA Details
      • Written Incident Timeline Narrative

      Disclaimer: Educational guide based strictly on MHA & State Police guidelines. Does not constitute formal legal advice.
